# Retail Contact Center Benchmarks 2025

KPI benchmarks for retail and e-commerce customer service call centers,
based on 187 reporting organisations.

## First Call Resolution (FCR)

Retail FCR tends to run higher than banking due to less complex transactions
(returns, order status, simple product questions).

- **Median FCR**: 78%
- **Top quartile (P75)**: 84%
- **Bottom quartile (P25)**: 71%

## Average Handle Time (AHT)

- **Median AHT**: 285 seconds (4m 45s)
- **Top quartile**: 230 seconds
- **Bottom quartile**: 360 seconds

Retail AHT is significantly lower than banking — fewer regulatory disclosures,
simpler product set.

## Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)

- **Median CSAT**: 85%
- **Top quartile**: 91%
- **Bottom quartile**: 79%

## Abandon Rate

- **Median abandon rate**: 5.4%
- **Top quartile**: 3.2%
- **Bottom quartile**: 8.1%

## Service Level (80/20)

- **Median service level**: 80%
- **Top quartile**: 87%
- **Bottom quartile**: 71%

## Agent Utilization

- **Median agent utilization**: 71%
- **Recommended target**: 70-78%

## Seasonality note

Retail benchmarks shift materially during peak season (Nov-Jan). The
numbers above are annual averages; if benchmarking against peak periods,
expect FCR to drop 3-5pp and AHT to rise 8-12%.

Source: Retail Customer Service Index 2025, NRF; ICMI Retail CC Benchmark.
