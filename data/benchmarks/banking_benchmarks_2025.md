# Banking Contact Center Benchmarks 2025

Industry-aggregated KPI benchmarks for retail and commercial banking call
centers. Data based on 142 institutions reporting to the Contact Center
Benchmark Consortium (CCBC), 2025 cohort.

## First Call Resolution (FCR)

Banking call centers handle disproportionately high authentication and
verification overhead, which suppresses FCR relative to other industries.

- **Median FCR**: 72%
- **Top quartile (P75)**: 79%
- **Bottom quartile (P25)**: 64%
- **Best in class**: 84% (Tier-1 institutions with skill-based routing)

Drivers of higher FCR: authentication automation, agent access to consolidated
customer view, branch-network knowledge integration.

## Average Handle Time (AHT)

Median AHT in banking trends higher than retail due to regulatory disclosures
and complex products (mortgages, investment accounts).

- **Median AHT**: 412 seconds (6m 52s)
- **Top quartile (P75 — lower is better)**: 358 seconds
- **Bottom quartile (P25)**: 487 seconds

Note: AHT alone is not a target metric — pairing it with FCR avoids the
"rushed call" failure mode.

## Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)

- **Median post-call CSAT**: 82%
- **Top quartile**: 88%
- **Bottom quartile**: 75%

Banking CSAT correlates strongly with first-contact resolution and authentication
friction. Institutions that streamline ID verification see CSAT lifts of 4-6 pp.

## Abandon Rate

- **Median abandon rate**: 6.2%
- **Top quartile (lower is better)**: 4.1%
- **Bottom quartile**: 9.8%

## Service Level (80/20)

Percentage of calls answered within 20 seconds.

- **Median service level**: 76%
- **Top quartile**: 84%
- **Bottom quartile**: 67%

## Agent Utilization

- **Median agent utilization**: 68%
- **Recommended target**: 70-75% (sustainable, accounting for after-call work)
- **Above 85%**: indicates risk of agent burnout and attrition

## ROI considerations

A 5pp improvement in FCR in a banking call center of ~120 agents handling
~4,200 calls/day typically yields $1.8M-$2.4M annual savings (reduced
repeat contacts, shorter total handle time per resolved issue, lower
churn from frustrated customers).

Source: CCBC 2025 Industry Report; cross-referenced with Gartner Contact
Center Performance Benchmarks Q2 2025.
