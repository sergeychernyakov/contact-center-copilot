# Cross-Industry Contact Center Benchmarks 2025

Aggregated benchmarks across all reporting industries (banking, retail,
telecom, healthcare, insurance, utilities). Use as a fallback reference
when industry-specific data is not available.

## First Call Resolution

- **Median FCR (all industries)**: 74%
- **Top decile**: 86%
- **Bottom decile**: 58%

Distribution is skewed: regulated industries (banking, healthcare,
insurance) tend to fall below the median; e-commerce and telecom self-service
augmentation pushes upper tiers higher.

## Average Handle Time

- **Median AHT**: 355 seconds
- **Standard deviation across industries**: ~110 seconds

## Customer Satisfaction

- **Median CSAT**: 83%
- **Top quartile**: 89%
- **Bottom quartile**: 76%

## Abandon Rate

- **Median**: 5.8%
- **Healthy range**: 3-7%
- **Action threshold**: > 8% indicates capacity or routing problems

## Service Level (80/20)

- **Median**: 78%
- **Industry-standard target**: 80% answered within 20 seconds

## Agent Utilization

- **Median**: 70%
- **Optimal sustainable range**: 65-75%
- **Above 85%**: predictive of attrition spikes within 60-90 days

## NPS

- **Median**: 35
- **Top quartile**: 52
- **Best in class**: 65+

## Cost per call

- **Median**: $5.40
- **Voice channel only**: $6.20
- **With self-service deflection**: $3.80

Source: Aggregate of CCBC, Gartner, ICMI, NRF benchmarks; 2025 calendar year.
